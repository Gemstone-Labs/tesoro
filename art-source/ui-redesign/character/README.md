## Review status

The completed generations are too tall and slender for the requested 3.5–4-head semi-chibi proportions. Their initial generation prompts requested that ratio, but the output did not meet it. Targeted correction calls encountered repeated network errors. Do not treat these drafts as the approved final proportions.

Take 1 also needs a more distinct three-quarter body rotation and correction of its back-view satchel strap direction. The two independently generated candidates have small garment/equipment variations beyond face and hair. Resolve these when locking the selected character. Gem outlines and small hardware should also be checked on the selected design.

Visual inspection shows friendly stylized explorer faces, real limbs/hands, no pirate hat, eyepatch, sword, gold coins or treasure chest. The hardware reads steel and the palette is largely dark teal/neutral with emerald, warm skin and muted brown leather. This is visual inspection, not a numerical palette or anatomy certification.

`readability.html` displays front-view crops at approximately 96px character height for small-size review. Browser capture failed twice, so readability has not been visually verified at that size and no screenshot proof is claimed.

## Files and provenance

Final-path sheets are 2048×2048 RGB exports, Lanczos-upscaled from native 1254×1254 generations. Native first renders are retained under `native/`. `QA.json` records dimensions and SHA-256 hashes. Built-in image_gen was used throughout; no API/CLI generation fallback was used.

Exact initial and corrective prompts, reference order and failure notes are in `../PROMPTS-log.md`. `PROMPTS.md` and `PROMPT-take-2.md` retain the initial character prompts locally.

No poses generated. After character selection and proportion approval, the requested transparent 1024×1024 poses are: welcome, explain, win, lose, waiting, empty, backing. The seven poses must all use the selected character exactly.

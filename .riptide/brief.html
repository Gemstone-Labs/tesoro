Protocol assessment — tesoro_core (Solana-native LST positions & advances)

Executive brief · simulation evidence over the declared region · Verdict: ready_to_send (declared)

1What we did

We ran Riptide — an economic stress-testing tool for Solana programs — as a guided-simulation sweep against tesoro_core (Solana-native LST positions & advances): sol_price_drop_bps sweep (lst.v1) completed 28 of 28 swept iteration(s) under a fixed-seed policy.

  • ›Risk objective: Native LST position solvency, yield-mint bounds, early-unlock enforcement, and Treasury instant-redeem buffer utilization under exogenous SOL/USD price shocks (0-60%)
  • ›Swept sol_price_drop_bps (sol price drop in basis points; 100 bps = 1.00%) across 0–6000.
  • ›This was a custom guided-simulation stress sweep, not a catalog scenario: Riptide varied the named axis as an applied stress across 7 stress cell(s) (4 iteration(s) per cell), then reported invariant failure rates from 28 completed iteration(s).
  • ›Lifecycle under test: open_native_position, take_native_advance, early_unlock_rejected, set_price_shock, request_instant_redeem.
  • ›A fired invariant is the declared risk signal for that stress cell; it is not audit signoff or a claim about untested flows.
  • ›Deterministic execution under a fixed-seed policy — reproduce byte-for-byte with the commands in section 5.

2What we found

  • ✓Held — open_native_position. No declared invariant fired under these inputs. Evidence is bounded to the declared, fixed-seed parameter region and run budget.

4Scope & limits

This assessment records simulation evidence observed within the campaign's declared, fixed-seed parameter region and run budget. It is evidence over that region only — not audit signoff, formal verification, complete protocol safety, or a prediction of mainnet behavior.

  • ›Oracle price updates are driven deterministically via tesoro_core::set_price; Pyth PriceUpdateV2 receiver staleness and confidence checks (take_native_advance_onchain) are out of scope of this sweep.
  • ›Simulated yield runs against the real sim_lst.so binary at fixed 7% APY / 4,380x speedup over a single 10 simSOL 2-month position and 0.12 simSOL seeded Treasury.
  • ›Cross-chain LayerZero V2 EVM vault messaging (PositionVaultOApp / tesoro_bridge) and SPL stake-pool slashing are verified separately by Foundry invariants and Rust ledger proptests.
  • ›take_native_advance_onchain (Pyth PriceUpdateV2) is out of scope: Out of scope for this sweep; oracle shock is driven via tesoro_core::set_price.

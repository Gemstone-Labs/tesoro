Three ways to wear emerald

Same screens, same Schibsted Grotesk, same emerald. What changes is how much of the product the gem gets to shape: its cut, its light, or its mass. In all three, the Gema mark is a small emerald-cut stone rather than a flat diamond glyph.

Sample screens with example figures: 1 Gema = $0.01 at issuance, and the entry ladder is 500 / 1,000 / 2,500. "Block Puzzle" is a working title. The gem tiles on the board show the idea of a puzzle twist, not a decided mechanic.

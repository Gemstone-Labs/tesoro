# Internal audit, 2026-10-16 (mainnet launch ticket 27)

Internal review of the programs frozen for audit. **Not an external audit.** Branch `ml/27-internal-audit`, base `f5795df`.

## Scope

- Solana: `tesoro_core` (every instruction: native and stake Positions, Advances and Extend, Redeem, Instant Redeem, Unlock, Treasury, Matches, Backing, Seasons and the payout window, Protections, Invite gate, entry sizes, on-chain Pyth prices, admin and config), `block_puzzle`, `holdem`, `trading_duel`, `tesoro_bridge` (inbound messages, peers, outbound sends).
- EVM: `PositionVault`, `PositionVaultOApp`, `StakeZap`, `WstETHRateAdapter`, `KHypeRateAdapter`, the mainnet deploy scripts. `SimLST` is test mode only.
- Server routes that hold or use keys (`app/src/app/api/**`, `app/src/server/**`) and the keeper's key handling.
- Out of scope: MagicBlock, LayerZero and Pyth themselves, the Squads and Safe contracts, the app's UI.

Threat model: a malicious Player, a malicious Backer, colluding Accounts, a stolen hot key (keeper, oracle, Invite, relayer), a griefer, MEV and front-running, price and rate manipulation, LayerZero message spoofing and replay.

## Method

1. Read the domain language, ADRs 0001-0012, `docs/tesoro-core.md`, `docs/yield-model.md`, `docs/cross-chain.md`, `docs/mainnet-keys.md`, `SECURITY.md` and the two earlier reviews.
2. Five independent read-only reviews, one per area (core money paths, matches/backing/seasons/protections/identity, the three games, bridge and EVM contracts, server routes and keys). Each finding was re-read in the code by the lead before it got a severity.
3. Known concerns from the ticket checked one by one (below).
4. For every Critical, High and cheap Medium: a failing test first (own commit, marked "red"), the fix (own commit), the test passing. On-chain behaviour checked on mainnet through read-only RPC.
5. `program_autofixer` on the changed program code: no issues.
6. Full suites re-run at the end (see "Verification").

Severity: Critical = loss of funds or unlimited mint by an unprivileged actor; High = loss or unbacked mint needing a precondition (a hot key, a rate move, a race), or a fairness break that extracts money; Medium = bounded loss, liveness loss or a trusted-role abuse; Low/Info = hardening.

Result: **0 Critical, 6 High (4 fixed, 2 need the founders), 14 Medium (1 fixed, 13 open; several are the founder decisions above), 13 Low (2 fixed), 6 Info.**

## High findings that need the founders (not fixed)

| ID | Finding | Why not fixed |
|---|---|---|
| E-1 | **Principal comes back short when the staking rate falls after yield was paid out** (EVM vaults, `PositionVault.sol` `_onUnlock` with `_recordPayout`; the same family on Solana stake Positions, F-S1 below). Yield is paid in shares that back the Principal, so after a fall the Position unlocks for fewer shares than its Principal. Example in the test: 10 wstETH shares, +10%, all net yield paid, rate back to the open rate, Unlock returns about 9.27 of 10 shares; the 0.18 fee shares in the vault do not close the gap. | Whoever bears it is a policy choice (below). Behaviour pinned by `test_policyPending_aRateFallAfterAPayoutLeavesPrincipalShort`. |
| G-2 | **Block Puzzle: the opener plays N runs and keeps the best.** `begin_run` works before a joiner exists, and `refund_match` lets the opener of an unjoined Match refund at once. The opener opens N Matches, plays them all, refunds the poor ones and leaves the best open: the joiner faces the maximum of N hidden scores for the cost of fees. `docs/games/block-puzzle.md` lists it as "not solved" but says nobody else is affected. | Every fix changes the product (below). |

## Decisions for the founders

1. **Rate fall after yield was paid (E-1, E-2, F-S1).** Options: (a) the Treasury absorbs it: Unlock tops the release up from the vault's spendable shares (fee shares, funding) up to the Principal, capped; (b) hold back part of the yield until Unlock (Redeem pays at most X% of accrued, rest at Unlock), so Principal always has a buffer; (c) socialise: the last Positions out bear it (today on Solana stake Positions); (d) accept and disclose. Recommendation: (b) with a buffer sized to a stressed fall, plus (a) for the remainder, and say plainly in the docs what is not guaranteed. Related: yield already applied to Debt on Solana is not taken back after an EVM dip (Principal and the vault Treasury are safe, `CumulativeDecreased` stops it going down), and after Unlock the vault freezes accrued yield at the dipped value while Solana may already have let holders redeem more (E-2): decide whether Solana unlock must wait for a fresh Yield Report.
2. **Instant Redeem that the vault cannot pay (B-1).** Chosen here: the payout waits for the Treasury (redeliver the LayerZero message). Alternative: build the refund (a per-send record in the bridge, a core instruction moving the Gemas back from the Treasury Account, and a rule for the case the Treasury has already redeemed them). It is bigger and has an unsolved edge, so it was not guessed. Whichever is chosen, tell the owner never to `withdrawTreasury` below what Solana recorded (B-2).
3. **Block Puzzle best-of-N (G-2).** Options: no opener refund once `begin_run` has been called, until the 24 h window ends; `begin_run` only after a joiner exists (removes play-before-pairing); one unpaired Block Puzzle entry per Account and entry size (needs a per-Account counter); a small cancel cost. Recommendation: no early refund after `begin_run` plus one unpaired entry per Account (the Invite gate makes Accounts scarce).
4. **Stale stake pool tolerance (core F2).** The program accepts a pool updated one epoch ago, so just after an epoch turn a payout converts at a rate about one epoch of growth (about 0.03% for jitoSOL) too low and pays that much extra in tokens; timeable by a Player. Requiring the current epoch removes it but blocks every exit for the hours until someone runs `UpdateStakePoolBalance` (many transactions, permissionless). Recommendation: keep one epoch, accept the leak, have the keeper run the update.
5. **Refund race after a deadline (core F3).** `refund_match` is permissionless the instant a deadline passes and `settle_match` has no priority: if a game's commit stalls (the MagicBlock TEE stall of 2026-10-13 is this case), the loser can refund first. Option: a grace period after the deadline in which only the game authority may settle or refund. Trade-off: slower liveness fallback.
6. **Fee ceilings (core F2b).** Platform plus Studio plus Leaderboard fees may total 50% of a Pot and the Leaderboard Fee is read at settlement. Option: hard per-fee caps (for example Leaderboard at most 3%, Platform plus Studio at most 15%) or snapshot the Leaderboard bps in the Match.
7. **Hold'em double no-show (game F-04).** If neither Player contributes entropy the Match is cancelled and both are refunded, even in a late hand; the Player behind can use this to erase a loss when the leader is also late. Option: from hand 2, forfeit the Player with fewer chips.
8. **Oracle on the web host.** `take_advance_onchain` requires the oracle as co-signer and rent payer, so the web host needs the Season-signing key for ETH/HYPE Advances, against `mainnet-keys.md`. Options: make the owner the payer and drop the oracle signature (program change), or a dedicated co-signer key set in `Config`.
9. **Season ordering (core F1, F5).** `record_season` takes any ended Season number in any order and each prize list is a share of the whole pool at that moment, with no deduction for earlier unpaid prizes. A stolen oracle key could record several Seasons that each promise 100% (the 48-hour void is the only defence, up to 10 transactions), and a keeper that was down for over a week would record two Seasons that cannot both be paid. Option: a `last_recorded` counter plus outstanding prizes subtracted from `pool_total`, and an admin `void_season`.

## Low and Info (not fixed)

- Low: L-1 signed requests carry no host or cluster binding; L-2 the age gate is client-side only (a self-attestation record, not enforced by the program); L-3 Season transactions are paid by the oracle outside the keeper spend cap; L-4 keeper `/healthz` binds all interfaces unless `KEEPER_HEALTH_HOST` is set; L-5 Turnstile turns off silently if one of its two keys is missing; L-6 email verify tokens never expire and verify is a GET; L-7 an Invite is burned before the Invite key is checked; B-5 peers cannot be revoked and `rate_bps` is unbounded; B-6 callers of `send_*` choose extra LayerZero options; E-5 anyone can open dust Positions for an EVM address; F6 `pay_season_rank` lets the caller choose which Sources a winner gets (trusted caller); F9-pool Pyth price is not conservative to the confidence interval.
- Info: Block Puzzle move timing is recorded but not enforced (ticket 24 asks for exactly that: bot review); hard-coded devnet `TEE_VALIDATOR` in the three games (make per cluster before mainnet); Trading Duel accepts exponents +8 and -8; Sanctum fork stake pool layouts have no fixture; mainnet needs `OnchainPrice` mappings for ETH and HYPE before the oracle lock; a Position over 18.4 ETH/HYPE cannot open; there is no vault pause or rescue (deliberate).

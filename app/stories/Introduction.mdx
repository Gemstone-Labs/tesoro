# Tesoro Storybook

Every piece of the Tesoro app, one at a time, with fake data. Nothing here talks to a chain, a wallet or the network.

## What is here

- **Foundations**: colours, type, the cut shape, the Gema mark and the icons, read live from the app's own CSS.
- **Brand**: the Facet T mark and the lockups, with every prop as a control.
- **Assets**: every image, loop and 3D file the app ships, listed from the manifests. New art shows up without editing a story.
- **Motion, Components, Games, Screens**: the app itself, from small parts to whole pages.

## The toolbar

- **Auth**: who is signed in on app screens. Signed in uses a demo Account. Signed out and "Signing in…" show the other two states.
- **Theme**: dark or light. It applies to the landing page, the docs, and stories marked as themed. The app shell is always dark.
- **Motion**: switches to reduced motion, the same as the system setting.
- **Guide**: turns the Guide character's tips on for app screens.

## Run it

```
pnpm --filter @tesoro/app storybook
```

It opens on port 6006. `pnpm --filter @tesoro/app build-storybook` makes a static copy in `app/storybook-static`.

## Where things live

- Stories: `app/stories/`, one folder per section. A story is a `*.stories.tsx` file; plain pages are `*.mdx`.
- Fake data and sign-in states: `app/stories/support/`.
- Module mocks: `app/.storybook/mocks/src/<path>` replaces `app/src/<path>` for every importer. Restart Storybook after adding a mock file.
- Shared bits for the Foundations, Brand and Assets galleries: `app/stories/kit/`.

The design rules behind all of it are in `DESIGN.md`.

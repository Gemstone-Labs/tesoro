# Motion

Every animation and transition in the app. Each story has timing controls (duration, easing, delay, repeat) and a Replay button, so you can watch and tune. Nothing changes the app: to keep a new timing, tell us the numbers.

Use the toolbar **Motion** switch to see Reduced motion. In the app, all animation stops or shortens under it.

## Not shown

- Games' own motion is in **Games** (Block Puzzle clears, the Hold'em clock, the Trading Duel grid).
- Short colour or opacity fades under 0.5 s on single landing elements (`.lp-nav`, `.lp-beat`, `.lp-meter`, `.lp-lane`, `.lp-stage-poster`) are listed in **Hover and focus** only where they respond to the pointer.
